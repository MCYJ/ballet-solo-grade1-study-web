A few things to know

Is this an official Royal Academy of Dance app?

No. RushLabs independently develops this study app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Royal Academy of Dance or any government body. The real award is a practical assessment; this app does not administer it or award a qualification.

Does the notebook approve a dance or a free choice?

No. A teacher-discussed category is a personal note. The app does not check eligibility, approve repertoire or prescribe choreography. Check the current provider rules and discuss your choices with your teacher.

Will it score or analyse my dancing?

No. Technique, Music, Performance and Recall are observation focuses you select for your own notes. The app does not record your dancing, analyse video, assign official grades or predict results.

Can I download it now?

Not yet. A paid download is planned with the included study material and notebook. No separate subscription is enabled. Final price and regional availability will be shown in the store when the app is released.
